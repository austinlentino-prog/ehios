Why Austin stopped making bracelets

In April 2026 Austin started making bracelets (they were rubber bands with a paperclip) to signal people were in the Enemy Hunters. But the same day Austin started making them, David lost his bracelet, and over the next few weeks almost everyone lost theirs. Austin didn't lose his — he stopped using it because the rubber band and paperclip broke. Christian used his paperclip for a badge he gave to Austin because he joined the Daily Detectives, leaving no one else with bracelets. That marked the end of the bracelet era.

If you ever want the bracelets back let me know, but it's very unlikely for me to start making them again after I shut down bracelet production.
