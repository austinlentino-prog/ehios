Why Austin Stopped Making Bracelets

During fifth grade, Austin frequently made jokes and comments toward Mr. Winston. Eventually, Mr. Winston reported the situation to social worker Mr. Zhao. Mr. Zhao then met with Austin to discuss the matter and address the concerns that had been raised.

Following that conversation, Austin declared a "global lockdown" within the Enemy Hunters community. During this period, all services were temporarily suspended, and the website was taken offline. Several weeks later, services gradually resumed, and the website, along with other projects and activities, returned to normal operation.
